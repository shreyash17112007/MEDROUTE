Prototype Privacy Policy
This policy describes the current MedRoute student prototype. It is intended to make the demo's data practices understandable; it is not legal advice and should be replaced with a production legal review before real patient deployment.

1. What information may be processed?

  • Account information such as login ID, name, role and hospital/dispatcher context.
  • Profile details you choose to enter, such as phone, email, age, address and emergency contact.
  • Emergency information such as incident type, location/pickup, destination hospital, status and journey information.
  • Operational information such as ambulance status, hospital/resource information and Green Corridor requests.
  • Location information when you enable GPS/location features or provide a location manually.
  • Consent and preference choices stored by the prototype.

2. Why is information used?

Information is used to authenticate users, coordinate emergencies, discover suitable hospitals, support ambulance and traffic coordination, process Green Corridor requests, show operational resources, and evaluate the prototype.

3. Who may receive emergency information?

Within the prototype, relevant emergency information can be visible to authorized roles such as the common user, dispatcher, hospital staff and traffic police when needed for the coordination workflow. Access is role-based in the interface; production deployment would require stronger identity, authorization and audit controls.

4. Third-party services

The prototype uses Supabase for selected database-backed functions and OpenStreetMap ecosystem services for mapping/location-related features. Requests to those services may contain the information necessary for the requested operation. Review their own policies before production use.

5. Where is prototype data stored?

Much of the demo state is stored in the browser using localStorage/sessionStorage. Selected data, including Green Corridor requests and hospital data, is connected to the MedRoute Supabase project. This prototype should not be used with real sensitive patient records.

6. Your choices

Optional location and analytics permissions can be declined. Consent preferences can be reviewed from Settings. Withdrawing consent may prevent some features from working; withdrawal does not by itself erase data already stored by the prototype.

7. Security

The prototype uses role-based interface controls and database access policies for selected Supabase tables. It has not undergone a production healthcare security assessment, penetration test, or clinical privacy certification.

8. Contact & complaints

For this student prototype, privacy questions should be directed to the MedRoute project team. A production deployment should publish a dedicated privacy contact/Data Protection Officer and a formal complaint process.

9. Policy updates

We may update this prototype policy as features change. The consent version and update date are shown on the consent screen.

Design note: this notice follows the general direction of India's Digital Personal Data Protection framework—clear notice, specified purposes and meaningful consent—but it does not by itself establish legal compliance.
